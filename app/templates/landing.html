What it's allowed to do

Two kinds of action, one rule: reads happen without asking, writes always ask first.

  • Reads

    Checks the diary, your price list, the caller's history. Mid-sentence, no asking.

  • Writes

    Books, cancels, refunds. Always confirms first, in one short question, and waits.

What Are AI Voice Agents?

AI voice agents are intelligent software programs that can hold natural phone conversations — listening, understanding context, and responding in real-time. Automite AI builds custom voice agents for businesses to automate calls, reduce overhead, and deliver consistent customer experiences.
